简体中文

「高见 TallSight」（以下简称"本应用"）高度重视用户隐私。本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 我们不收集任何数据
本应用无需注册、无需登录，不连接开发者的任何服务器，不嵌入任何统计、分析或广告组件。 您创建的孩子档案、身高体重记录、骨龄记录及预测结果保存在您的设备本地； 如您开启 iCloud 同步，数据仅通过您本人的 iCloud 账户（Apple CloudKit 私有数据库）在您的设备间同步， 我们（开发者）无法访问，也不会向任何第三方提供、出售或传输。

2. 儿童信息
本应用面向家长/监护人使用。由于数据仅存储于您的设备与您本人的 iCloud 空间、不经开发者传输， 我们不会在线收集任何儿童个人信息。请勿让儿童在无人指导下自行管理设备数据。

3. iCloud 同步与备份（如适用）
iCloud 同步与导出的备份文件均由 Apple 以您的 iCloud 账户加密保护，受 Apple 隐私政策约束，开发者无法读取。

4. 数据删除
您可随时在应用内删除孩子档案（将连带删除其全部记录），关闭 iCloud 同步或卸载本应用后，数据即被彻底清除。

5. 未成年人
本应用不面向 14 周岁以下儿童单独提供服务，不在网络上收集其个人信息。

6. 政策更新
如政策发生重大变更，将在应用更新说明中公示。

7. 联系我们
如有任何疑问，请通过 App Store 本应用页面下方的「App 支持」入口与我们联系。

English

TallSight (Height Predictor, "the App") takes privacy seriously.

1. We collect no data. The App requires no account, connects to no developer servers, and embeds no analytics or advertising components. Child profiles, measurements, bone-age records and predictions are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is synchronized only through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database). The developer cannot access it and never shares, sells or transmits it to any third party.

2. Children's information. The App is intended for use by parents/guardians. Because all data stays on your device and your personal iCloud space, we do not collect children's personal information online.

3. iCloud sync & backup (if applicable). iCloud sync and exported backups are encrypted and protected by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot read them.

4. Deletion. You may delete a child's profile (with all records) in the App at any time; turning off iCloud sync or uninstalling the App permanently erases all data.

5. Minors. The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not collect their personal information online.

6. Changes to this policy will be announced in App release notes.

7. Contact: please reach us via the "App Support" link on the App Store page.
